## 📁 Featured Projects

### 1. [Financial & E-Commerce Fraud Early Detection Analysis](./02_Financial_Fraud_Early_Detection/)
* **Stack:** `Excel` | `SQL` | `Python (Scikit-Learn)` | `Power BI` | `Tableau`
* **Business Problem:** Payment fraud prevention across 10,000 credit/debit card transactions ($1.55M volume).
* **Deliverables:**
  * **Excel:** Automated rule engine screening simulator & False Positive vs. Fraud Loss tradeoff matrix.
  * **SQL:** Relational Star Schema, rolling hourly velocity window functions, and "Impossible Travel" supersonic speed queries.
  * **Predictive Modeling:** Balanced Random Forest Classifier (PR-AUC `0.9928`, ROC-AUC `0.9998`, Recall `98.5%`) scoring fraud probabilities and risk tiers.
  * **Power BI:** 3-page Operations Command Center, 10+ DAX measures, and daily triage review queue.
  * **Tableau:** Cross-border geospatial burst map, dual-axis attack timeline, and Level of Detail (LOD) formulas.
* **[Read Full Fraud Case Study ➔](./02_Financial_Fraud_Early_Detection/)**
