Featured Projects

### 1. [SaaS & Telecom Customer Churn & Retention Analytics](./01_SaaS_Telecom_Customer_Churn/)
* **Stack:** `Excel` | `SQL` | `Power BI` | `Python (Scikit-Learn)`
* **Business Problem:** A subscription service is losing $1.64M annually to customer attrition.
* **Deliverables:**
  * **Excel:** Power Query ETL, 24-Month Cohort Retention Matrix, What-If Revenue Impact Calculator.
  * **SQL:** Relational Star Schema (`dim_customers`, `dim_contracts`, `dim_services`, `fact_subscriptions`), Window Functions, and Customer Success outreach rankings.
  * **Predictive Modeling:** Random Forest Classifier (ROC-AUC `0.731`) scoring every account with a churn probability and risk tier (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`).
  * **Power BI:** 3-page interactive executive dashboard with 15+ DAX measures and What-If parameter simulation.
* **[Read Full Project Case Study ➔](./01_SaaS_Telecom_Customer_Churn/)**
